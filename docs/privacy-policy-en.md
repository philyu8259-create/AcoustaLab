# AcoustaLab Privacy Policy

AcoustaLab 1.4 is designed as a local acoustic utility. We respect your privacy and keep the app focused on signal generation, audio routing, built-in test workflows, local measurement, and calibration reporting.

## Data We Collect and Use

AcoustaLab does not upload your generated audio, microphone audio, presets, test history, calibration results, or reports to our servers.

The app does not include third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs for ad or behavior tracking, App Tracking Transparency prompts, or commercial tracking code paths.

## Microphone Access

AcoustaLab may request microphone access for acoustic calibration and local audio measurement features. Microphone input is used on device for measurement-related functionality. We do not upload microphone audio to our servers.

## App Data Stored on Device

The app may store local settings such as preferences, trial status, calibration state, route information, built-in test history, preset data, and saved reports on your device. This information is used to keep features stable across launches.

## Test History, Reports, and Export Behavior (v1.4)

- Test history and calibration report records are stored in the app sandbox.
- Exported calibration reports or test artifacts are written from local data only.
- The app does not automatically upload exported files to any remote endpoint.
- If you share an exported file through system share flows, transmission is controlled by your operating system and your explicit choice.

## In-App Purchases

AcoustaLab uses Apple In-App Purchase for one-time full access unlock. Purchase transactions and payment processing are handled by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

AcoustaLab is provided without subscriptions.

## Support Email

If you contact us for support by email, we will use the information you provide only to respond to your request.

## Children

AcoustaLab is a utility app and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## Changes

We may update this privacy policy when the app changes. The latest version will be available at this page.
